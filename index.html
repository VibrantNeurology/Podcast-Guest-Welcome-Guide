The world of Left Brain Third Eye

About the show

Left Brain Third Eye is a place for the mystically curious to explore the unseen and have it all make sense. It's a combination of energy updates, direct channels, teachings around building intuition, and interesting conversations with people in the field. My hope is that this podcast sparks expansive insight and more joy for listeners.

Growing together

The intention

These conversations are meant to be mutually supportive and beneficial, for both of us and for all of the listeners too.

With that in mind, I'll be promoting each episode, including on:

  • Instagram ↗
  • YouTube ↗
  • Newsletter ↗

Since these conversations are meant to support both of us, I'd love for you to share the episode with your people as well. I'll invite you as a collaborator on the Instagram posts, and I'm happy to send you a few clips to use in your stories, newsletter, or anywhere else that feels right.

A little direction, plenty of room

How the conversation might flow

Our time together is a chance to explore spirituality, intuition, the unseen, and healing through your experiences and perspective. The questions below are simply guidelines to give us a head start. I welcome the conversation to flow naturally, and I’m excited to see where it takes us! If there are any questions you’d love for me to ask, feel free to send them my way.

  1. 01

    Introductions.

    I'd love for you to introduce yourself and your work, if that's applicable, and share a little bit about who you are.

  2. 02

    Your journey.

    I'll probably ask about your journey, and anything you want to share about your spiritual awakening, psychic awakening, or anything else you feel is relevant to help listeners get to know you.

  3. 03

    Spirituality and intuition.

    Some more theoretical questions: how you define spirituality and intuition, how it's helped you in your daily life, any practices you follow, and maybe more modern ways your intuition shows up for you. This helps listeners get a sense of how it can apply to their own daily lives.

  4. 04

    Our sessions (optional)

    If you're a previous client and feel comfortable sharing, I'll ask about your journey with our sessions. This helps give people clarity around what a session might look like, since that can feel a little vague before someone goes on their own journey, especially if they're new to the healing world.

  5. 05

    The floor is yours.

    Anything else you want to talk about, or any offers you have coming up, I'd love to give you the floor and let people know where they can find you.

  6. 06

    What’s bringing you joy?

    One of the goals of the podcast is to spark joy, so I’ll ask what’s bringing you joy these days and what you’re doing for fun.

Room to relax and be yourself

A little reassurance

If you’re feeling a little nervous, please know that the podcast will be edited to remove bloopers, filler words, or any parts you don’t want included. My hope is that knowing this helps you feel more at ease and lets our conversation flow naturally.

Thank you again so much for being on this journey. I look forward to speaking with you and mutually supporting each other.
